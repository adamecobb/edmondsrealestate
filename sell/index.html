Selling in Edmonds: common questions

When is the best time to sell a home in Edmonds?

Spring usually brings the most buyers, but inventory is also higher then. Well-priced homes sell in every season, and a listing with less competition can do very well. Adam can show you how your neighborhood has performed month by month.

Should I remodel before selling?

Usually not a major remodel. Paint, lighting, landscaping, repairs, and staging tend to return the most. Adam will walk through your home and tell you what is worth doing and what is not.

Should I buy first or sell first?

It depends on your finances and risk tolerance. Adam can explain the options, including bridge financing, rent-backs, and contingent offers, and connect you with lenders who work with these situations.

How much does it cost to sell?

Commission is negotiable and is always discussed up front. Other costs typically include Washington real estate excise tax, title and escrow fees, and any preparation you choose to do. Adam will give you an estimated net sheet before you list.
