Home valuation FAQ

Is the home valuation really free?

Yes. There is no cost and no obligation. Many Edmonds homeowners request one every year to track their equity.

How is this different from Zillow or Redfin estimates?

Automated estimates cannot see your view, your remodel, the feel of your street, or the condition of your home. Adam compares your home with recent Edmonds sales he knows firsthand and adjusts for what drives value here.

How quickly will I hear back?

Most requests get a personal reply within one business day. For a more precise number, Adam may suggest a quick walk-through.

Do I have to sell?

No. Plenty of people request a valuation to plan ahead, refinance, or satisfy curiosity. Adam is happy to be a resource whenever you are ready.
