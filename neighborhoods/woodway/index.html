About Woodway

Woodway is a small, separate town on the southwest edge of Edmonds, known for large wooded lots, estate homes, and a quiet, private setting on the Puget Sound bluff. Residents have Edmonds mailing addresses and are minutes from downtown Edmonds.

Why people love it

  • Large, heavily wooded lots and a private feel
  • Minutes from downtown Edmonds and the waterfront
  • Served by Edmonds School District schools
  • Some western properties have dramatic Sound and Olympic views

Homes in Woodway

Estate homes, custom builds, and mid-century homes on large lots. Luxury properties here need a marketing plan built for high-end buyers.

A great fit for

  • Luxury buyers
  • Privacy seekers
  • Custom home builders

Woodway FAQ

Is Woodway part of Edmonds?

No. Woodway is its own incorporated town with its own government, but it shares Edmonds mailing addresses and schools and is part of daily Edmonds life.
