About Downtown Edmonds & The Bowl

The Bowl is the natural amphitheater of hillside streets that slopes down to downtown Edmonds and Puget Sound. It is the heart of town: tree-lined blocks, sweeping Olympic Mountain views, and a walkable core of independent shops, restaurants, galleries, and waterfront parks. Buyers compete hard for homes here, and well-prepared listings in The Bowl consistently draw strong attention.

Why people love it

  • Walk to the Edmonds–Kingston ferry, the Sounder commuter rail station, and the waterfront
  • Saturday Edmonds Summer Market, the Third Thursday Art Walk, and the Edmonds Arts Festival
  • Brackett's Landing, Marina Beach, and the Edmonds Underwater Park
  • Edmonds Center for the Arts and a busy lineup of downtown restaurants and coffee shops
  • Yost Park’s wooded trails and seasonal pool just east of downtown

Homes in The Bowl

Classic Craftsman and mid-century homes, newer custom builds on view lots, and a strong condo and townhome market close to the water. View orientation, walkability, and parking are big value drivers here.

A great fit for

  • Downsizers who want to walk everywhere
  • View buyers
  • Seattle and ferry commuters
  • Buyers who want a true small-town downtown

The Bowl FAQ

Why do homes in The Bowl hold their value so well?

Limited inventory, protected views, and walkability to the waterfront and downtown create steady demand. There are only so many homes within walking distance of Main Street, and buyers know it.

Are there condos in downtown Edmonds?

Yes. Downtown has a large share of the city’s condos, from older waterfront buildings to newer boutique projects. HOA health, reserves, and rental rules vary a lot, so review each building carefully.

What are homes selling for in The Bowl?

Prices in The Bowl change month to month and vary a lot with views, lot size, and condition. Request the free The Bowl market report or a personal home valuation for current numbers.
