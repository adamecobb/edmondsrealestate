About Seaview

Seaview is a quiet, established neighborhood north and east of The Bowl. Many streets on its western side have Puget Sound and Olympic views, and the whole area is a short drive to downtown, the ferry, and Highway 99. It is a favorite for buyers who want an Edmonds address with a bit more yard.

Why people love it

  • Seaview Park’s playground and open fields
  • A few minutes by car to downtown Edmonds and the waterfront
  • Close to Olympic View Drive for quick access north to Perrinville and Meadowdale
  • Mature trees and larger lots than the downtown core

Homes in Seaview

Mostly single-family homes from the 1950s through the 1980s: ramblers, split-levels, and daylight basements, with a growing number of remodels and new builds on view lots.

A great fit for

  • Growing families
  • Buyers who want space and views without downtown prices
  • Remodel and value-add buyers

Seaview FAQ

Does Seaview have view homes?

Yes, many homes on the western slopes have Sound and mountain views. The quality of the view, whether it is protected, and the sight lines from the main living areas all have a big effect on value.
