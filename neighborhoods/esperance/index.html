About Esperance

Esperance is an unincorporated area of Snohomish County that is completely surrounded by the city of Edmonds. It has Edmonds mailing addresses and the same schools and shopping as nearby Edmonds neighborhoods, but county government handles land use and many services. That can mean different rules, and often a different price point.

Why people love it

  • Central location near Five Corners and Highway 99
  • Quiet residential streets with established trees
  • Close to Lake Ballinger and Edmonds shopping districts
  • Edmonds School District schools

Homes in Esperance

Mostly mid-century single-family homes on generous lots. Some buyers are drawn here because county land-use rules can differ from the city’s.

A great fit for

  • Value-minded buyers
  • Remodel and investment buyers
  • Buyers who want yard space

Esperance FAQ

What does "unincorporated" mean for an Esperance homeowner?

Snohomish County, not the City of Edmonds, handles permitting and some services. Taxes, utilities, and building rules can differ, so check what applies to any home you are considering.
