Only in Edmonds

Why people fall for Edmonds, and stay

Ferries on the Sound, seals on the beach, and a fountain at the heart of downtown. Here's the Edmonds life that buyers are paying for.

Ferry to Kingston

Watch the ferries come and go from the waterfront, or ride across to the Kitsap and Olympic Peninsulas.

The heart of downtown

The fountain at 5th & Main anchors a walkable downtown of shops, galleries, restaurants, and the Saturday Summer Market.

Seals & sea life

Harbor seals rest on local beaches, and Edmonds Underwater Park, next to the ferry dock, is a favorite for divers.

Salmon country

Local creeks and the Edmonds Marsh are part of ongoing work to restore habitat for returning salmon.

Life at the marina

The Port of Edmonds marina sits beside Marina Beach, with boats of every size and a waterfront walkway.

Beaches & sunsets

Brackett's Landing, Marina Beach, and Meadowdale Beach Park, with sunsets over the Olympics almost every clear evening.
